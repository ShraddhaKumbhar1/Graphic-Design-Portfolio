Halden Coffee

An identity for a neighbourhood café opening its second shop.

Role
Identity, guidelines, print
Client
Café and roastery, 30 seats
Discipline
Brand identity
Year
2026

The brief

Halden’s first logo came from a menu template. It blurred on cup sleeves, could not be printed in one colour and had no rules for the new shop’s signage.

Delivered

  • Logo suite in 4 forms
  • Palette of 3 colours
  • Type pairing
  • 24-page guidelines
  • Cups, bags, cards and menu
  • Shopfront sign

Why it works

The italic wordmark holds at 12 mm on a bag sticker and at 2 m on the shopfront, so the café needs one logo, not three.
