Ossa Skincare

A packaging system that lets eight products share one shelf without blurring together.

Role
Packaging, dielines, print production
Client
Skincare startup, 3 people
Discipline
Packaging
Year
2026

The brief

Ossa launched with eight products in near-identical bottles. Customers in shops picked up the wrong one, and reorders by email kept asking “which serum was it?”

Delivered

  • Carton and label system for 8 products
  • Dielines for 3 formats
  • Print specifications
  • Two rounds of press proofs

Why it works

A large product number lets a customer find their serum from two metres away and reorder it without reading a word.
