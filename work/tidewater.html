Tidewater Quarterly

A redesign of a 64-page quarterly so long essays are calm to read and photographs get room.

Role
Art direction, grid, templates
Client
Independent coastal magazine
Discipline
Editorial design
Year
2025

The brief

Tidewater’s editor was laying out each issue from scratch. Essays ran in narrow columns, photos were cropped to fit and no two spreads lined up.

Delivered

  • 12-column grid and baseline
  • Master pages and paragraph styles
  • Cover template
  • Issue 14 layout
  • 10-page style guide

Why it works

Body text at 9.5/13 pt with about 62 characters per line, and captions on the same baseline, so spreads line up across the gutter.
